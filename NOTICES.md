## Aarti recordings

No third-party aarti recordings are bundled yet. Om Jai Jagdish Hare, Jai
Lakshmi Mata and Jai Ganesh Deva currently use generated placeholder audio
(`src/audio/aarti/placeholders/`), which is original to this project. It
must be replaced before release.

The search record, rejected candidates and steps for adding an approved
recording are in [`docs/aarti-recordings.md`](docs/aarti-recordings.md).
